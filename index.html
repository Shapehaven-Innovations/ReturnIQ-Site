The blind spot

Most stores know their return rate. Almost none know why.

A refund tells you a sale reversed. It doesn't tell you the sizing ran small, the photos oversold the color, or that the same handful of customers send back half of what they buy. That "why" is exactly where your margin goes - and it's already sitting in data you have.
